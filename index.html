Star Wars Lore: From The Acolyte to Episode VI

The Acolyte (High Republic Era)

A dark mystery-thriller set ~100 years before The Phantom Menace. A former Padawan reunites with her Jedi Master to investigate brutal crimes against Jedi, uncovering a shadowy dark-side threat that ends with the first live-action appearance of Darth Plagueis the Wise.

Episode I – The Phantom Menace (32 BBY)

The Trade Federation blockades Naboo. Qui-Gon Jinn and Obi-Wan Kenobi discover young Anakin Skywalker on Tatooine. Qui-Gon believes Anakin is the Chosen One. Darth Maul is defeated, but the Sith have returned.

Episode II – Attack of the Clones & The Clone Wars (22 BBY)

Anakin and Padmé fall in love and secretly marry. The Clone Wars begin. The animated series follows Anakin, Obi-Wan, and Ahsoka Tano fighting across the galaxy against the Separatists.

Episode III – Revenge of the Sith (19 BBY)

Chancellor Palpatine manipulates Anakin’s fear of losing Padmé. Anakin turns to the dark side, becomes Darth Vader, and helps execute Order 66. Obi-Wan defeats Anakin on Mustafar. The Empire rises.

The Dark Times (19 BBY – 0 BBY)

Obi-Wan lives in exile on Tatooine watching over Luke. Andor shows the early Rebellion. Star Wars Rebels follows a small crew that becomes vital to the growing Alliance against the Empire.

Episode IV – A New Hope (0 BBY/0 ABY)

Luke Skywalker joins Obi-Wan Kenobi, Han Solo, and Leia Organa to rescue the princess and destroy the first Death Star.

Episode V – The Empire Strikes Back (3 ABY)

The Empire hunts the Rebels. Luke trains with Yoda on Dagobah. Darth Vader reveals he is Luke’s father.

Episode VI – Return of the Jedi (4 ABY)

Luke confronts Vader and the Emperor on the second Death Star. Vader redeems himself, kills the Emperor, and saves his son. The Empire falls and the galaxy celebrates.
